About Probal Manobsheba Foundation

Probal Manobsheba Foundation (PMF) is a non-profit voluntary humanitarian organization committed to helping vulnerable communities through humanity, transparency, volunteerism and sustainable development.

🎯

Our Mission

To serve humanity through blood donation, disaster response, food support, education and community development.

👁️

Our Vision

To build a compassionate Bangladesh where everyone receives support in times of need.

❤️

Our Values

Humanity, Integrity, Respect, Accountability, Volunteerism and Sustainable Development.
