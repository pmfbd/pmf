About PMF

Probal Manobsheba Foundation (PMF) is a non-profit voluntary humanitarian organization dedicated to helping vulnerable communities through social service, emergency response, youth engagement and sustainable development.

🎯 Mission

Helping people through humanity, volunteerism and transparency.

👁 Vision

Creating a compassionate society where nobody is left behind.

❤️ Values

Humanity • Unity • Service • Respect • Accountability
